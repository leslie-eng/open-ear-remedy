# Open Ear

Open Ear is an emotional-support service: people talk to a trained human Listener on a call, or to an AI Companion in chat, and pay in Kenyan shillings with Credits or a Monthly Pass.

## People

**Caller**:
A signed-in person who starts a Call to talk to someone.
_Avoid_: client, customer, patient

**Listener**:
A person an admin has approved to take Calls from the Listener Console. Listeners see only a Caller's Display Name.
_Avoid_: agent, counsellor, therapist, operator

**Display Name**:
The name a Caller chooses to show Listeners; "Anonymous" when they have not set one.
_Avoid_: username, full name

**Admin**:
A staff member who manages Listeners, ebooks, Call Reports and Credit Adjustments.

## Calls

**Call**:
A live, unrecorded voice conversation between one Caller and one Listener, held in the browser.
_Avoid_: session, phone call, ticket

**Ringing**:
The time a Call waits for a Listener to accept it; it is offered to every available Listener and the first to accept takes it.

**Connected Time**:
The part of a Call from when audio first connects until it ends; the only part that is charged.
_Avoid_: duration, talk time

**Cooldown**:
A short quiet period after each Call during which a Listener is not offered new Calls.
_Avoid_: break, pause

**Call Report**:
A Listener's account of a Call that went wrong, sent to Admins for review.
_Avoid_: complaint, flag

**Listener Console**:
The page where a Listener goes online, sees Ringing Calls, and takes them.

## Paying

**Credit**:
The unit people spend on Calls and AI messages. Credits never expire.
_Avoid_: token, coin, point

**Credit Pack**:
A fixed bundle of Credits bought once for a fixed price in shillings.
_Avoid_: bundle, plan, top-up

**Monthly Pass**:
A 30-day purchase that includes a fixed amount of Call time and a daily allowance of AI messages, spent before any Credits. It does not renew by itself.
_Avoid_: subscription, membership, unlimited plan

**Free Trial**:
A one-time gift of Credits to an account with a verified email.

**Credit Adjustment**:
An Admin's manual change to someone's Credits, always with a written reason.
_Avoid_: refund, correction, bonus

**Offer**:
Anything that can be bought: a Credit Pack or the Monthly Pass.
_Avoid_: product, SKU

## Other

**AI Companion**:
The AI that chats with people in text. It is not a therapist and points anyone in danger to Crisis Help.
_Avoid_: bot, AI therapist, assistant

**Crisis Help**:
The page listing emergency numbers and helplines for people in immediate danger.
_Avoid_: SOS, emergency page

**Ebook**:
A downloadable self-help book from the Open Ear library.
